## Description draft in English

UMBRAL helps adults prepare their first household socioeconomic classification request in Chiclayo, Peru. It explains the official process, document conditions and next steps through a five-step guide, a preparation checklist, dated official references and a printable sheet. Spanish and English content can be read offline after the first complete load. It does not collect identity documents, calculate classification, submit applications or guarantee benefits.

Kiro created the initial React/TypeScript implementation, Specs, Steering and initial QA. Codex independently reproduced failures, corrected content and software defects, added regression and accessibility checks, and prepared publication. We do not claim unobserved Agent Hooks, field validation or measured social outcomes. Exact evidence and limits are in the attribution and audit report.
