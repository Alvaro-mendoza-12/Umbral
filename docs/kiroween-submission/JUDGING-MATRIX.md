# Judging matrix

The [official rules](https://kiroween.devpost.com/rules), reviewed **11 November 2026**, identify five equally weighted criteria: **Potential Impact, Technological Implementation, Design, Innovation/Idea and Presentation**. Each is **20%**. This matrix maps evidence and limits to those criteria; it assigns no predicted score.


The jury's questions differ by criterion: Potential Impact concerns a credible audience/problem and usefulness; Technological Implementation examines effective, thoughtful Kiro use; Design concerns a coherent, intuitive product; Innovation/Idea considers creativity and difference from existing concepts; Presentation concerns a clear demonstration of the working solution. The evidence and gaps below answer those questions without assigning scores.

| Criterion | Evidence to show | Current weakness or limit | Demo approach |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Potential Impact — 20%** | A clear preparation problem; audience of students, jobseekers, learners, entrepreneurs and households; concrete opportunities, conditions and local route tools | No human usability sessions, adoption data, successful applications or measured time/visit savings. Country/category coverage is uneven and editorial freshness requires maintenance. | Explain one person's information need, then show benefit, key condition and a practical preparation step. Describe intended impact, not proven outcomes. |
| **Technological Implementation — 20%** | Working React/TypeScript PWA; typed catalogue/status rules; independent country/language state; local persistence, export/print; 49 unit, 484 local E2E and 86 production E2E passes; successful main CI | Static architecture is intentionally limited; no live institution feed or automatic eligibility decision. Kiro depth is evidenced by initial implementation and authentic Specs/Steering, without evidence for every possible Kiro feature. Later work is Codex's. | Demonstrate persistence and route progress; briefly show the original three Specs/five Steering artifacts and explain the Kiro/Codex boundary precisely. Link recorded tests rather than reading totals at length. |
| **Design — 20%** | The Illuminated Threshold: night palette, warm threshold, arches, gateway cards, progress; early save after key condition; full eligibility before official application; responsive and keyboard-aware UI | Theme-to-function rationale has not been validated with people. Halloween treatment is restrained; automated accessibility does not certify full conformance. | Begin on the real home, move through a gateway card and preparation progress, then show mobile. Explain the intended role of visual cues without a measured-improvement claim. |
| **Innovation/Idea — 20%** | Connection between source-backed discovery and a personal preparation route; country/language separation; CSE guidance carried into a broader bilingual companion | Novelty relative to competing products has not been independently established. The app is not a new AI model, live matching engine or automated application service. | Demonstrate the connected workflow and information distinctions. Avoid “first,” “unique” or unsupported superiority claims. |
| **Presentation — 20%** | English primary README and description; working URL, public repository/MIT, screenshots, testing instructions and truthful attribution | A public YouTube URL and actual Devpost submission remain owner-dependent until recorded. English media must remain within the official duration requirement. | Use real application footage, readable captions and one coherent journey. End with the product/repository URLs and evidence. Verify the public video opens before pasting its URL. |
