## Main project description / About the project

The following content is ready to paste as Markdown if the form supports it. It is a condensed version of the complete English project description.

### Inspiration

Finding an opportunity is only the beginning. People still need to understand who it is for, what it offers, what conditions apply and how to prepare. UMBRAL addresses that gap for students, jobseekers, learners, entrepreneurs and households. It began with guidance for Peru's household socioeconomic classification procedure, CSE, and now applies the same preparation approach to an international bilingual catalogue. “Umbral” means threshold: discovering a possibility should lead to an informed next step.

### What it does

Choose a country, explore six categories and open specific opportunities with benefits, key conditions, eligibility, restrictions, costs, dates and official sources. English and Spanish are independent of country selection. The 11 November 2026 editorial snapshot contains 31 concrete opportunities plus Peru's CSE procedure, including 15 jobs across Peru, the United States, Mexico, Colombia and Spain. International entries are shown only for listed eligible countries. The snapshot includes closed records; it is not a claim that every offer is open.

Save an opportunity after reading its benefit and key condition. In My Route, mark preparation steps, add personal tasks, track progress, download text and print. Full eligibility remains available before the official application step. Saving does not apply or establish eligibility. The Peru CSE entry includes an early guide link, five-step guidance, eleven preparation checkboxes, resources and FAQs. UMBRAL does not calculate or request CSE, award benefits or replace a ULE.

The installable PWA supports cached reading after a first complete online load; external official websites need a connection. Preparation state stays in the current browser, without an account, cloud sync or recovery service.

### How we built it

React, TypeScript and Vite power a static application, with React Router, i18next/react-i18next and Vite PWA/Workbox. Typed editorial records separate facts and sources from availability rules, country filtering, local state and presentation. Browser localStorage holds route/checklist progress. Vercel serves the build. No runtime AI API or application backend is required.

Kiro created the initial MVP, including the CSE experience, bilingual implementation, PWA, initial tests and reports. Its original three Specs and five Steering files remain in `.kiro` with unchanged content. Codex independently audited and corrected the app, added accessibility/regression work, expanded the catalogue and international experience, implemented the later theme and interaction fixes, and prepared publication. We do not attribute those later changes to Kiro or claim unobserved Agent Hooks, MCP, Crew or property-based testing.

### Category: Costume Contest

The Illuminated Threshold uses a mysterious night palette, a warm threshold, arch icons and gateway-like cards to connect discovery with action. My Route extends that metaphor through preparation progress. The owner approved this visual direction. We demonstrate its functional rationale in the working interface without claiming measured usability improvement or a strong horror experience. Category acceptance remains the organisers' decision.

### Challenges

We needed to expose the benefit and key condition early while keeping full eligibility available before the official application action. Country and language needed separate state so translation would not imply wider access. Keeping external information current is an ongoing editorial task: published deadlines and overdue review dates can close or hide actions, and some job reviews are due on 14 November 2026 and become unconfirmed the following day if not refreshed. Sources need rechecking before submission and judging.

### Accomplishments

The public product combines specific opportunities, dated sources, bilingual country-aware discovery, a local preparation route and the retained CSE guide. Recorded checks on 11 November 2026 include 49 unit tests, 484 local E2E passes and 86 production E2E passes at mobile and desktop widths, plus successful main CI. These demonstrate tested software behavior, not user outcomes or full accessibility certification.

### What we learned

A saved opportunity is an intention, not an application. A translated page is not a change in jurisdiction. A source-review date is evidence, not a promise of continued availability. Keeping these distinctions visible matters as much as expanding the catalogue.

### What's next

Reverify catalogue sources before submission and judging, conduct real usability sessions, improve the flow from observed results and expand verified country/category coverage. Editorial maintenance and preparation recovery deserve further work. No human interviews, successful applications or measured time savings are claimed yet.
