# Problem and solution

## Human problem

A programme name or search result rarely answers every practical question a person needs before acting: the benefit, intended audience, location, access conditions, cost, deadline and first preparation step. Information may be split across institution pages or expressed in administrative language. Remembering what to do after finding it creates another burden.

UMBRAL addresses that preparation gap for students, jobseekers, learners, entrepreneurs and households. The initial concrete case was Peru's CSE procedure. The current product applies the same method to specific opportunities across five countries, with qualified international entries. This problem framing has not been validated by interviews or a controlled comparison.

## Product response

| Need | Shipped response | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Find a relevant starting point | Country selection, six categories and catalogue filters | Coverage is curated and uneven; some categories have no matching records. |
| Understand an offer | Benefit, audience, key condition, detailed eligibility, restrictions, dates and source links | Documentary review is not an eligibility decision. Unpublished facts stay labelled. |
| Keep an opportunity for later | Save after the benefit/key condition; My Route holds local entries | Saving does not apply or prove eligibility. |
| Prepare an action | Source-backed steps, personal tasks and progress | Completion represents preparation, not institutional approval. |
| Keep a usable copy | Text download, print and cached reading after initial online load | External sites need internet; local state can be lost. |
| Understand CSE in Peru | Early guide, five steps, eleven checkboxes, resources and FAQs | No CSE calculation/request, register access, benefit award or replacement of the ULE. |

## Journey

Choose a country → discover an opportunity → understand the benefit and key condition → save if useful → review full eligibility and official conditions → prepare in My Route → continue at the institution's official site.

Country and language remain separate throughout. Selecting English does not make an opportunity internationally accessible. CSE guidance remains Peru-specific, and Chiclayo office information remains district-specific.

## Evidence and open questions

The deployed app and automated checks demonstrate software behavior, not impact. The editorial snapshot reviewed on 11 November 2026 has 31 concrete opportunities plus CSE, with closed and unconfirmed records excluded from default discovery. Some reviews are due on 14 November 2026 and become unconfirmed the following day if not refreshed; sources need refreshing before submission and judging.

No human usability sessions, reduced visits, successful awards, job placements or measurable time savings are established. Future sessions should test whether people understand the benefit, correctly identify a disqualifying condition, save and recover a route, and distinguish preparation from application. Results should be recorded before making outcome claims.
