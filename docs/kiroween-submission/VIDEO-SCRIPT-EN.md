# English demo script

Title: UMBRAL — The Illuminated Threshold. Target: 160 seconds (2:40), below 3 minutes. Production source: 5c77941542c1b498c37639a2d03a26dd8fbfee42. English narration uses locally installed Microsoft Zira Desktop. No music.

## 0–20s — The problem

Capture: Actual production catalogue and a real vacancy; no invented search engine or filmed actor. Information dispersion is explained by narration.

Narration: Finding opportunities should not be complicated. Yet information is scattered across institutions, portals, and eligibility rules. People need to know where to look, what a listing requires, and which step to take next.

## 20–45s — A clearer threshold

Capture: Live home, independent language/country controls; switch US and back to Peru.

Narration: UMBRAL is an international opportunity companion, built to turn that uncertainty into a clearer path. Choose a country independently of your language. The initial catalogue covers five countries, alongside international options. The Illuminated Threshold uses warm light and gateways to make the interface feel calm and recognizable.

## 45–75s — Discover and understand

Capture: Jobs category, real Concentrix customer-service vacancy, eligibility, complete requirements, conditions, dated official sources. No application is sent.

Narration: Choose Employment, then open a specific vacancy. Each card highlights its location, benefit, and a key requirement. This example asks for completed secondary education and basic computer skills. Read the full requirements and conditions before applying. Unknown details stay unknown. Review dates and availability are editorial, and the official source remains the place to confirm and apply.

## 75–100s — Prepare in My Route

Capture: Save, open My Route, mark a step, add a fictitious task, reload, download preparation.

Narration: Save an opportunity in My Route without submitting an application. Your preparation stays in this browser, without an account. Mark a preparation step and add a simple task. Reloading keeps this local progress. You can download or print your plan. These marks describe preparation, never approval or eligibility.

## 100–120s — Mobile guidance

Capture: Actual 390 × 844 screen: early Save, direct CSE home link, guide and next step.

Narration: On mobile, Save is easy to reach beside the summary. The conditions and complete requirements remain available. In Peru, a direct CSE entrance leads to the five-step guide and checklist. UMBRAL neither classifies households nor submits the official request.

## 120–145s — Authentic Kiro development

Capture: Read-only viewer of the eight actual repository files. This is not a recreated Kiro IDE session.

Narration: Kiro built the initial React and TypeScript MVP through original specifications and steering documents. These are the actual requirements, design, tasks, and five steering files, preserved in the repository. Codex later audited, corrected, tested, and evolved the international catalogue and visual experience. The application does not call an AI model at runtime.

## 145–160s — A brighter next step

Capture: Actual production home and CSE guide, ending on the home view. The final message is narration/subtitles, not a fabricated product feature.

Narration: This is a working product, with automated checks and honest limits. Human usability sessions are still pending. UMBRAL. Real opportunities. Clearer paths. A brighter next step.

Synchronization: subtitles-en.srt follows measured narration clip durations. Real browser footage is edited into this timeline; short navigation waits are retained. Mobile footage is scaled proportionally on the same canvas. A dedicated lower caption strip does not cover the interface. Kiro footage shows original files, not an invented Kiro session. The opening uses real browser navigation instead of an actor or fabricated multi-portal screen.
